Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 2026

LabMage ("we", "our", "the app") is a physics simulation game developed by Aamir. This policy explains what data is collected when you use the app.

1. Data We Collect

We do not collect or store any personal information ourselves. The app stores all game data locally on your device and does not transmit it anywhere.

However, the app uses Google AdMob to display advertisements. AdMob may collect:

  • Device or other IDs — the Android advertising ID
  • IP address — may be used to estimate general location
  • User product interactions — app launches, taps, video views
  • Diagnostic information — app performance, crash logs, energy usage

This data is collected and shared with Google AdMob and its third-party advertising partners for advertising, analytics, and fraud prevention purposes. Learn more at https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites.

2. How We Use Data

We do not use your data. AdMob uses it solely to serve ads.

4. Data Deletion Request

We do not store any personal data on our servers, so there is nothing for us to delete. However, if you wish to have any data associated with your device removed, you have two options:

  • Reset your advertising ID: Open Android Settings → Google → Ads → Reset advertising ID. This disconnects your device from previously collected ad data.
  • Request deletion by email: Send an email to aamirshaik0912@gmail.com with the subject "Data Deletion Request" and we will forward your request to Google AdMob within 7 days.

5. Your Privacy Rights

Processing is based on your consent, which you can withdraw at any time by adjusting your device settings or using the "Manage Privacy Settings" option in the app.
  • California, USA: California residents have the right to know what personal information is collected and to request its deletion. Contact us at aamirshaik0912@gmail.com.
