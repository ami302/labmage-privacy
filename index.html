Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 2026

LabMage ("we", "our", "the app") is a physics simulation game developed by Aamir. This policy explains what data is collected when you use the app.

1. Data We Collect

We do not collect or store any personal information ourselves. However, the app uses Google AdMob to display advertisements. AdMob may collect:

  • Device or other IDs — the Android advertising ID
  • IP address — may be used to estimate general location
  • User product interactions — app launches, taps, video views
  • Diagnostic information — app performance, crash logs, energy usage

This data is collected and shared with Google for advertising, analytics, and fraud prevention purposes.

2. How We Use Data

We do not use your data. AdMob uses it solely to serve ads.
